Interactive Story Rough Draft: Deep Blue Decision

My interactive story is called Deep Blue Decision. The premise is about a hungry shark swimming through the deep ocean and choosing between three different paths: following a school of fish, following a human diver, or investigating a strange glowing light coming from a hidden shipwreck. The story takes place in the open ocean near coral reefs and a sunken ship. The main character is the shark, while the fish, the diver, and the treasure act as the main points of conflict and decision-making.

The first path is the School of Fish. The shark can either attack quickly or wait patiently. If it attacks too fast, the fish scatter and the shark may only catch a few, leading to a small survival ending, or lose them completely and be redirected toward the treasure path. If the shark waits patiently, it can perform a perfect strike and reach the Great Feast ending, or another predator may appear, forcing the shark toward the human diver path.

The second path is the Human Diver. The shark sees a lone diver near coral reefs and must decide whether to attack directly or observe first. If it attacks immediately, the diver panics and either escapes, leading to the Missed Opportunity ending, or a boat appears and pushes the shark toward the treasure path. If the shark observes first, it notices the diver discovering something shiny and can either follow the diver to the treasure path or leave and return to the fish path.

The third path is the Hidden Treasure. A glowing light from a shipwreck attracts the shark. The shark can either enter the shipwreck or circle outside first. If it enters, it may find the treasure chamber and reach the Treasure Victory ending, where it claims the deep ocean as its kingdom, or it may become trapped inside the wreck and reach the danger ending. If it circles outside, it can spot either the diver or the fish again, which reconnects the story to the other two main paths.

This structure allows multiple paths and multiple endings, with different choices leading back to earlier branches. This makes the story non-linear and gives the user several different ways to reach each ending. The goal of the story is to let the user decide whether the shark chooses survival, risk, or discovery, while exploring how each decision changes the outcome.
