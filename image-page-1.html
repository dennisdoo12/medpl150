1920 × 1080 Image

I chose this image because the no parking sign is horizontally oriented, which made the 1920 × 1080 resolution a perfect fit for the composition. I made sure the depth of field was deep so the sign and surrounding environment stayed sharp, and the camera settings, including the aperture, were adjusted to maintain clarity and detail throughout the image. I also made sure to have the sign as the center of the photo because that is what I wanted the subject of this image to be.
