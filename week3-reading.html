Week 3 reading

Article: With ‘AI slop’ distorting our reality, the world is sleepwalking into disaster

In this article, the author describes how AI-generated content has created a second reality that exists alongside real photographs and footage of the world. One channel shows actual events such as politics, war, and daily life. The other is filled with what the author and many people call “AI slop” which ranges from fantasy girlfriend images to political propaganda and hyper idealized portrayals of men and women. Some things that stood out to me is that the problem is not simply fake images but the distortion and overload that these images create. The more AI-generated things that we as a society consume, the harder it becomes to emotionally process what's real.

The political dimension of AI is really concerning. The article explains how AI is being used to create rightwing fantasies and images of idealized white families and pushing political narratives. Since AI is trained on existing data, it often reproduces biased ideas about race, gender roles, and power structures. We need to remember that not everything that AI presents to us is considered the norm and harmful ideologies can be spread from consuming too much of this. Something that this article does not dive deep into is the dangers of AI deception. People can use AI to fake crimes and can potentially try to get other people in trouble. If AI continues to develop and people begin to be unable to distinguish real and false photos or images, court systems would potentially have to be careful when taking in evidence.

Another point that resonated with me was about how AI content spreads through messaging platforms like WhatsApp. These messages cannot be easily fact checked or challenged and older relatives may trust AI generated images because they come from somebody they know personally. Older people need to continue to stay informed about the dangers of AI and always have verification from trusted sources when giving people their personal data. AI also desensitizes people due to the infinite amount of generation. AI images are constantly being generated and you can virtually design anything you want. While this may have some perks, an issue that people may face is that it will raise a generation of desensitized children that get bored really easily and need constant rapid stimulation mentally. The emergence of social media such as Tik Tok and Instagram are already bad enough but now with the emergence of AI, people will constantly view this content especially as it gets better and more accurate. With the rise of chatbots as well, people need to be reminded of the dangers of getting too deep into AI and being able to distinguish AI from reality.

Overall, this article suggests that the dangers of AI is not only deception but distorting reality. If we start to be unable to distinguish real from fake, we will begin to distrust our own perception and overtime our morals can change as well. The responsibility of people to always critically evaluate images is more important than ever and people need to make sure that they proceed with caution and continue to ask questions when it comes to anything in their daily life.
